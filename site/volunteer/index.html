Meet the volunteers

The hands that build this project.

  • Garth Warner Founder, General Maintenance

    55 From New Zealand, Fell in love with Peru 9 years ago and found a home where I can be of service to others.

  • Patrick De Vries Customs Agent, Electrician

    Patrick De Vries is 55 and comes from the Netherlands. He is in Peru to explore the culture.

    He works in customs and logistics, and he is a handyman: electrician, carpentry and plumbing.

    Helping people is in his nature. He loves the idea of working on something new that benefits others.

  • Pamella Garcia Cyber Security, Web Design

    Pamella Garcia is 31 and comes from Brazil. She has lived in Peru for 6 years because it feels like her new home.

    She is in charge of web design and software engineering for the project's apps.

    What she likes most about working on the project is that it is an initiative that comes from the heart, and from the wish to connect people with nature in a very beautiful way.

  • Nello Fraboni Musician, Plant Care

    Age 46 French American horticulturist passionate about plants, nature and sustainable agriculture.

    He has extensive experience in plant cultivation, propagation and greenhouse management.

    Currently in Peru, he is exploring permaculture and tropical growing systems.

    You will experience the duality of how correct care of seeds, plants and animal life deeply relates to ourselves.

  • Matias Lopez: Chef, Forestry Technician

    Matias Lopez is 38 and comes from Argentina. He is in Peru to travel and to know new places. He is a Forestry Technician and a chef with a specialty in breads.
    He volunteers because the project is beautiful and the intention behind it is heartfelt.

  • Leticia (Hampi Mama)

    From Cancha Cancha. A powerful plant medicine Practitioner.
    Based in Huaran....Hampi Mama work shopI wanted to volunteer and support this project because I wanted to share my ancestry knowledge with the world.

  • Omar

    34 Omar is from Urubamba.A professional local Backhoe operator and Dump truck contractor.
    An expert in his field of work and very honest guy.
