Inka Braille Trail App

This app brings the trail to blind and visually impaired people.

It guides the visitor through the tour. At each station it tells the story of that place, so the walk can be followed by listening.

The path stays within reach of someone who cannot see it. Each stop has a voice, and the story of the station shows the way.
