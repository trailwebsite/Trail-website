Trail 2: Aromatic and Medicinal Path

On this path you learn to identify and use the plants. All downloaded as you go to use in the future.

Inscreva-se como voluntário

Conheça mais sobre:

Download the app: Then the experience begins.Feel or see the Braille Map layout from beginning to end
Two options:
1: Visually Impaired: Audio version
2: SIghted: QR/NFC scan at stations..
All Junction points have Braille information cards.
Visually Impaired: Audio version

On this sensory path you will experience the many wonders and aromas the Apus (Mountain Spirits) and Pachamama (Mother Earth) nourish us with. There will also be QR/NFC markers provided with detailed information about individual plants, traditional, medicinal, culinary and personal care uses and how to prepare them.All this information you will be able to take away with you so you can refer to it at any time.
The main reason is that I personally have visited many information trails and wasn't able to retain the information or i wrote things down and lost the information over time, but now you can refer to it when ever needed.
Enjoy :)

Na trilha sensorial, as pessoas são convidadas a caminhar devagar e encontrar este lugar com o corpo. Um corrimão de corda marca o caminho. Há pedra, madeira, folhas e flores para tocar. As ervas e a terra úmida oferecem aroma. A água, os pássaros e o vento oferecem som.

O chão muda sob os pés, da pedra para a terra, e cada passo diz onde você está. Nada nesta caminhada depende da visão.
