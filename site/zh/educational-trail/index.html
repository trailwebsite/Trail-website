Trail 1: Educational Path

在这里，你顺着生命的循环学完整条路。

报名成为志愿者

进一步了解：

Download the app: Then the experience begins.Two options:
1: Visually Impaired: Audio version
2: SIghted: QR/NFC scan at stations..

All Junction points have Braille information cards.
1. Visually Impaired: Audio version
You will have a trained volunteer who will accompany you if you do not have a sighted guide. The guided audio will take you on a journey through 20 stations of information. There will be 4 rest points along the way. At each rest point you will have to answer 5 questions before moving to the next.A hand rail will help guide you along the path. A ball at the end of each hand rail will signify a change of hand rail from right to left or left to right. White cane detectable stone edges shall guide your direction on the path.A bell at the end of each rail will signify an easy to find seating area to answer your Q&A.

Global Impact: Looking at new and old data to prepare for changes
Water Management: Water testing, Identifying problems, New solutions, Cleaning and maintenance, Preserve and protect.
Replanting: Cycles of harvesting, environmental help
